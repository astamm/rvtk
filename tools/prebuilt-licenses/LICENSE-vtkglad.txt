glad (VTK bundled OpenGL loader) - MIT + Khronos
================================================

glad is VTK's bundled OpenGL/EGL loader generator output (headers under
ThirdParty/glad/vtkglad; built as libvtkglad). It is redistributed inside the
pre-built VTK libraries shipped by the rvtk project (extracted into
inst/prebuilt from the vtk-<version>-*.tar.gz/.zip release archives).

The license texts below are reproduced verbatim from the `LICENSE` file
shipped in VTK's ThirdParty/glad/vtkglad source tree at the v9.7.2 tag (commit
0558d4d4e4bc86c4b4bf7af2f2488011a4de9e0f; source:
https://github.com/Kitware/VTK).
